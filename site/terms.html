1. What ZTO is

ZTO is a desktop application for solo app developers ("the Software"), made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). It helps you manage app store listings, accounts, and related workflows. It runs locally on your computer.

2. Purchases and licensing

Official builds are sold through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Your payment, invoice, and applicable taxes are handled by Lemon Squeezy, and the charge may appear under their name. A purchase grants you a license key for personal or internal business use of the official builds. The source code is separately available under a source-available license — building and using it yourself, for yourself, is free and requires no key.

3. Trials and subscriptions

Official builds include a free trial (currently 3 days of full functionality, starting when you first connect a store). Subscriptions (ZTO Plus) renew automatically until cancelled; you can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the paid period. Refunds are covered by our refund policy.

4. Store actions are yours

ZTO can perform actions against your Google Play and App Store Connect accounts — editing listings, uploading assets, updating in-app purchases. Some of these actions are irreversible on the store side. ZTO always shows you what it is about to change and requires your explicit confirmation before applying. By confirming, you take responsibility for the change, and you remain responsible for complying with Google's and Apple's terms for your developer accounts.

5. Your data

ZTO has no server for its core features. Your account inventory, store credentials, and app data stay on your computer. Passwords are stored only in your operating system's keychain and are never transmitted to us. If you use ZTO Plus hosted AI, your prompts are relayed through our proxy to an AI model provider to generate a response; we do not store the content of these requests. License keys are verified directly against the Lemon Squeezy API.

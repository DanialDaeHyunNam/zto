1. What ZTO is

ZTO is a desktop application for solo app developers ("the Software"), made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). It helps you manage app store listings, accounts, and related workflows. It runs locally on your computer.

2. Purchases and licensing

Official builds are sold through Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record. Your payment, invoice, and applicable taxes are handled by Lemon Squeezy, and the charge may appear under their name. A purchase grants you a license key for personal or internal business use of the official builds. The source code is open source under the Apache License 2.0 — building and using it yourself is free and requires no key. Official builds (signed, notarized, auto-updating) are what a purchase licenses.

3. Trials and subscriptions

Official builds include free use of the full app for a limited period (currently 3 days, starting the first time you launch an official build). After that, paid features are locked until you enter a license key; your account inventory stays accessible either way. Subscriptions (ZTO Plus) renew automatically until cancelled; you can cancel any time and keep access until the end of the paid period. Refunds are covered by our refund policy.

4. Store actions are yours

ZTO can perform actions against your Google Play and App Store Connect accounts — editing listings, uploading assets, updating in-app purchases. Some of these actions are irreversible on the store side. ZTO always shows you what it is about to change and requires your explicit confirmation before applying. By confirming, you take responsibility for the change, and you remain responsible for complying with Google's and Apple's terms for your developer accounts.

5. Your data

ZTO has no server for its core features. Your account inventory, store credentials, and app data stay on your computer. Passwords are stored only in your operating system's keychain and are never transmitted to us. If you use ZTO Plus hosted AI, your prompts are relayed through our proxy to an AI model provider to generate a response; we do not store the content of these requests. License keys are verified directly against the Lemon Squeezy API.

6. Deleting your data

Because your data is stored locally, removing the app does not remove your data — it stays in ZTO's folder on your computer. To erase it, open Settings → Your data → Delete all local data in the app: this deletes your accounts, saved passwords, access history, store snapshots and caches, and signs you out of the embedded browser. Your license key and free-period record are kept so the app can still tell what you are entitled to; use Remove in Settings to unregister the device. The operating system keychain entry that encrypts your passwords cannot be removed by the app, but once the encrypted file is gone it can no longer open anything; the app shows the command to remove it yourself. Since we hold no account for you, there is nothing on our side to delete — apart from purchase records held by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record.

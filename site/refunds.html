Refund Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

We'd rather you try ZTO before paying — official builds include a free 3-day full-feature trial, and you can always build from source for free. If you bought it and it isn't working out, here's how refunds work.

One-time purchase (ZTO — $5)

14-day money-back guarantee. Email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund you in full, no questions asked. Your license key will be deactivated after the refund.

Subscriptions (ZTO Plus)

You can cancel any time from the Lemon Squeezy customer portal (linked in your receipt email); you keep access until the end of the paid period, and no further charges are made. If you were charged and haven't meaningfully used hosted AI in that billing period, email us within 14 days of the charge and we'll refund it. Renewal charges after substantial usage are generally not refundable, but if something went wrong — billing mistakes, double charges, the product broke for you — write to us and we'll sort it out.

How to request

Email libertas.kr@gmail.com from the address you purchased with, or reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy (our merchant of record), so approved refunds are issued through them and typically appear in 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer.
